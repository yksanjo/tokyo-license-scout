🗼 TOKYO LICENSE SCOUT

Day -/16 Your catalog 0 Rival catalog 0

Small Japanese IP owners are the opportunity.

You are scouting the backstreets of Nekomachi, a fictional Tokyo neighborhood, for small IP owners who need a US licensing partner. A rival agency is working the same streets.

Move one block per day by clicking a glowing tile. Find the six owners hidden in the grid. When you find one, make an offer: lowball and they may walk, offer fair and they usually sign, go generous and they always sign but your margin shrinks. The rival signs anyone they reach first. Sixteen days. Build the best catalog.
